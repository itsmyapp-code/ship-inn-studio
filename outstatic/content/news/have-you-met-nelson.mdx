If you’ve visited us at The Ship Inn, there’s a good chance you’ve already met our friendly ginger pub cat, Nelson! 🧡

Nelson is a bit of a local celebrity and everyone seems to love him! He belongs to Miguel and Michell and is very much their precious fur baby. 🥰🐾

Nelson likes to pop in from time to time, have a little wander around, say hello to our guests and make sure everything is running smoothly. He may not be here every day — he comes and goes as he pleases — but when he does make an appearance, he’s always happy to receive a fuss!

So next time you’re at The Ship Inn, Porlock Weir, keep your eyes peeled… you might just get to meet Nelson, our favourite ginger visitor!
