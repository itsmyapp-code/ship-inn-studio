Client Guide: Website Events & Analytics Dashboard

Overview: All custom events on the website are tracked privacy-friendly and automatically reported in real time inside your Vercel Analytics dashboard under the Events tab (as well as Google Analytics 4 if configured).

1. Event Reference & Dashboard Guide

Below is every event tracked across the website, where it originates, and what custom parameters are saved with it:

Event Name in Dashboard Trigger / What Fires It Key Data Recorded
Phone Call Click Visitor clicks any telephone link (tel:) on the website. location (header, footer, contact page, rooms CTA), phone
Direct Email Click Visitor clicks any email link (mailto:) on the website. location (footer, contact page, legal pages), email
Google Maps Directions Click Visitor clicks to open driving / walking directions to Porlock Weir (TA24 8PB). location, destination
Hero CTA Click Visitor clicks either the "From the Cabins" or "Contact us" buttons on the Homepage Hero banner. button_name, target_url
Rooms Feature Click Visitor clicks the "Explore Our Rooms" highlight card on the Homepage. target_url: "/rooms"
Pub & Restaurant Feature Click Visitor clicks the "View Food & Menus" highlight card on the Homepage. target_url: "/food-drink"
Things to Do Feature Click Visitor clicks the "Discover Things to Do" highlight card on the Homepage. target_url: "/things-to-do"
Menu PDF Click Visitor clicks to open or download any PDF menu on the Food & Drink page. menu_title (e.g. "Main Menu", "Sunday Lunch", "Wine List"), menu_url
Promo Banner Click Visitor clicks the Saturday BBQ / Braai promo banner or "Explore BBQ" button. promo_name, target_url
Article Click Visitor clicks an Upcoming Event card or Latest News item on the News & Events page. content_type (event/news), title, slug
Gallery Interaction Visitor clicks a category filter button (e.g., "Food & Drink", "Rooms") or clicks to open a photo lightbox. action (filter / lightbox_open), label
Contact Form Submission Visitor submits the main contact enquiry form. enquiry_type (general, room booking, table reservation), has_phone
Newsletter Signup Visitor subscribes to the Ship Inn email newsletter. page

2. How to Read Your Vercel Analytics Dashboard

1. Top-Level Events Table Shows the total count and unique visitor count for each specific event during your chosen date range (e.g. Last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days).
2. Drill-Down Filter Clicking any event row (such as Menu PDF Click or Phone Call Click) filters the whole dashboard to see which pages or dates generated those clicks.
3. Property Inspection Custom properties (like which menu was opened or which enquiry type was submitted) appear in the event breakdown views.

3. Top Insights to Watch

  1. Food vs Stay Demand: Compare Rooms Feature Click vs Pub & Restaurant Feature Click to see seasonal interest shifts between dining and overnight stays.
  2. Most Popular Menus: Look at Menu PDF Click to see if guests are checking out daily specials, Sunday roasts, or drinks before arriving.
  3. Direct Conversion Volume: Add together Phone Call Click + Contact Form Submission + Direct Email Click for total high-intent leads generated.
Need Custom Tracking Added? If you launch a new seasonal campaign, special accommodation package, or external booking engine, additional custom events can be added at any time.
