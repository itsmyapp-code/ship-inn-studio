Client Quick Guide: How to Upload & Manage Menus

⚠️ File Format Requirement: Downloadable menus must be in .pdf format. If your menu was created in Word, Canva, or Pages, please export or save it as a PDF before uploading.

Method 1: Uploading a New Menu PDF (1-Minute Method)

Use the dedicated Menu Manager tool on your website to upload your new menu PDF directly from your computer:

1 Open the Menu Uploader

In your browser, visit: https://www.theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk/admin/menus

2 Select the Menu

Click on the menu you want to update (e.g., Lunch Menu, Evening Menu, Breakfast Menu, or Sunday Lunch Menu).

3 Enter Season & Choose PDF File
  • Season / Subtitle: Enter the season or serving times (e.g., Autumn 2026 or Served 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM).
  • Browse PDF File: Click "Browse PDF file" and select your .pdf file from your computer.
4 Click "Upload & Publish Menu"

Click the blue upload button. Within 1 to 2 minutes, the live website will be updated with your new downloadable PDF menu!

🔄 How Does This Connect to the Outstatic Menus Page?

The Outstatic Menus tab and the Menu Uploader work together automatically:

  • Instant Sync: Whenever you upload a PDF via /admin/menus, it automatically updates the corresponding document inside your Outstatic Menus dashboard.
  • What Outstatic does: Outstatic acts as the master record. Inside Outstatic, you can:
    • Temporarily Hide a Menu: Change the Status from Published to Draft (and click Save) to hide it from the website.
    • Change Menu Order: Edit the Display Order number (1 = Breakfast, 2 = Lunch, 3 = Evening, 4 = Sunday).
    • Add Notes / Highlights: Add descriptions or notes to any menu.

Summary: When to use which tool?

Task Best Tool Link
Upload a new PDF menu Menu Manager /admin/menus
Hide / unpublish a menu temporarily Outstatic (Draft mode) /outstatic/menus
Change serving times or descriptions Either tool /admin/menus or /outstatic
