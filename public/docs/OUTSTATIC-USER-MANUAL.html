1. Dedicated Fast Admin Tools (1-Minute Updates)

1.1 Menu Manager & PDF Uploader

Use this dedicated tool whenever you have a new season or updated menu PDF:

  1. Open your browser and go to: https://theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk/admin/menus
  2. Click the menu you want to update (Breakfast Menu, Lunch Menu, Evening Menu, Sunday Lunch Menu, or Custom Menu).
  3. Enter the Season / Subtitle (e.g. Autumn/Winter 2026 or Served 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM).
  4. Click "Browse PDF file" and choose your .pdf file.
  5. Click "Upload & Publish Menu". The live website and Outstatic update automatically in 1–2 minutes!

1.2 Gallery Manager & Photo Uploader (with Auto-AVIF & WebP Conversion)

Use this dedicated tool to upload new photos to the gallery or delete old ones without having to manually resize images or copy file links:

  1. Open your browser and go to: https://theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk/admin/gallery
  2. Choose your upload mode:
    • Single Photo Upload: Add an individual photo with a custom title and caption.
    • Batch Upload: Upload multiple photos (3–10+ at once) into a chosen category.
  3. Click the matching category button (🍽️ Food & Drink, 🪵 Interior & Bar, 🏡 Exterior & Garden, 🛏️ Rooms & Cabins, 🌿 Surroundings & Exmoor, or ✏️ Custom).
  4. Click "Browse Image" to select your photo (supports .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .avif, .jfif, .gif up to 20MB) and verify the instant preview thumbnail.
  5. Enter a descriptive Photo Title / Alt Text and optional Caption.
  6. Click "Upload & Publish Image". Your photo is automatically converted into both .avif and .webp, published live, and synced with Outstatic CMS!
  7. Deleting or Editing Existing Photos: Scroll down to the Current Gallery Photos grid at the bottom of /admin/gallery to filter by category, click 🗑️ Delete to remove a photo immediately, or click ✏️ Edit in Outstatic.

3. Understanding the Outstatic Dashboard

The left sidebar of your Outstatic dashboard provides access to all content sections:

  • Pages: Controls text and banners on core website pages (Home, Food & Drink, Contact, Rooms, Things to Do, Gallery, News & Events).
  • Menus: Shows all uploaded menus (where you can toggle Draft/Published to temporarily hide a menu).
  • Gallery: Shows your photo collection (where you can edit captions or toggle Draft/Published).
  • Events: Add upcoming live music, pub quizzes, and seasonal celebrations.
  • News: Post articles and announcements.
  • Media Library: Central repository for all images used on website pages.

4. How to Edit and Save Any Page in Outstatic

  1. In the left sidebar, click Pages, then click the page you want to update.
  2. Focus on the Right Sidebar fields. (The large centre editor is not used for page layouts).
  3. If a field shows "+ Create", click it to open the text input box.
  4. Ensure Status is set to "Published" (not Draft).
  5. Click the black "Save" button in the top-right corner.
  6. Wait 1–2 minutes, then refresh your live website to see changes.

6. How to Add a New Event or News Article

  1. Click Events or News in the left sidebar of Outstatic.
  2. Click the black "New" button in the top right.
  3. In the centre screen: Type your headline in the Title box and write your story or event description below it.
  4. In the right sidebar:
    • For Events: Fill in eventDate (e.g. 2026-12-31), eventTime (e.g. 8:00 PM), location (e.g. The Ship Inn Bar), upload an image under Upload image (or Cover Image), and add a short Description. (Note: Past events automatically hide from the website once their date has passed!)
    • For News: Select the Published At date, upload a Cover Image, and enter a short Description.
  5. Set Status to "Published" and click Save.

7. Automatic Image Optimization (.avif & .webp) — How It Works

✨ Every Photo Uploaded Is Automatically Optimized

You never need to convert or compress photos before uploading them to the website:

  • Via the Gallery Manager (/admin/gallery): When you upload any .jpg, .png, or .webp photo, the server automatically resizes it (max 2000px width) and generates both an ultra-fast .avif version and a .webp fallback version.
  • Via Outstatic CMS (/outstatic): When you choose a .jpg or .png image inside Outstatic, it is automatically converted in your browser to .webp before saving, and the website build automatically creates its matching .avif version.
  • On the Live Website: Every image on the site uses a smart <picture> tag that serves .avif first with a automatic .webp fallback for older browsers.

8. Progressive Web App (PWA) & Offline Coastal Mode

The Ship Inn website is a full Progressive Web App (PWA) designed for visitors exploring Porlock Weir and the Exmoor coast:

  • Installable on Any Phone or Computer: Visitors can tap "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone/iPad Safari) or "Install App" (Android Chrome / Desktop) to install The Ship Inn with its own app icon.
  • Quick-Action App Shortcuts: Long-pressing the installed app icon gives instant 1-tap shortcuts to Menus & Dining, From the Cabins, and Contact & Directions.
  • Automatic Offline Caching: Once a visitor views your pages or opens your PDF menus, they are cached on their phone so they continue to work even if mobile signal drops along the coast path.
  • Offline Coastal Fallback Card: If a visitor loses signal while trying to open an uncached page, a branded offline screen displays your telephone number (01643 863288 with a 1-tap call button), address (Porlock Weir, TA24 8PB), and bar/food serving hours.
