# The Ship Inn, Porlock Weir — Client Guide: How to Upload & Manage Gallery Photos

This guide explains how to upload, organize, and manage photos on The Ship Inn website gallery.

---

## 🚀 Method 1: Using the Gallery Manager (Recommended)

Use the dedicated **Gallery Manager** tool on your website:

1. **Open the Gallery Manager:**  
   In your browser, go to: `https://www.theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk/admin/gallery`
2. **Choose your Upload Mode:**
   * **Single Photo Upload:** Perfect when you want to add an individual photo with a custom title and caption.
   * **Batch Upload:** Great for dropping 3–10 photos at once into a specific category.
3. **Select a Category:**  
   Click the category button that best fits your photo:
   * 🍽️ **Food & Drink**
   * 🪵 **Interior & Bar**
   * 🏡 **Exterior & Garden**
   * 🛏️ **Rooms & Cabins**
   * 🌿 **Surroundings & Exmoor**
   * ✏️ **Custom Category** (enter any name you like)
4. **Choose your Image File:**  
   Click **"Browse Image"** (or drag & drop). Supports `.webp`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, and `.avif`.
5. **Enter Title & Optional Caption:**  
   * **Title / Alt Text:** (e.g., *“Fresh Local Crab Sandwich”* or *“Log Fireplace in the Bar”*).
   * **Caption:** (e.g., *“Served with homemade crisps and side salad”*).
6. **Click "Upload & Publish Image":**  
   The image is uploaded, synchronized with Outstatic CMS, and goes live on the website gallery!

---

## 🔄 How Does This Connect to Outstatic CMS?

The **Outstatic Gallery collection** and the **Gallery Manager** are synchronized:

* **Automatic Sync:** Every photo uploaded at `/admin/gallery` automatically creates a corresponding document in your **Outstatic Gallery** collection (`/outstatic/gallery`).
* **Managing Photos in Outstatic (`/outstatic/gallery`):**
  * **Temporarily Hide a Photo:** Change **Status** from `Published` to `Draft`, then click **Save**. This hides it from the website immediately without deleting it.
  * **Edit Descriptions / Alt text:** Update captions or titles anytime.
  * **Delete a Photo:** You can delete photos either from the manager at `/admin/gallery` or directly in Outstatic.
