Adding Facebook Reels to the Website

The Ship Inn Porlock Weir — Customer Workflow Report & Options

📋 Executive Summary There are two straightforward ways for the customer to put Facebook Reels onto theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk:
  • Option A (Built-in /admin/reels Manager — £0/mo): Customer copies a Reel link from the Facebook app on their phone and pastes it into /admin/reels (~20 seconds).
  • Option B (Automatic Facebook Feed Widget): One-time 2-minute Facebook login; after that, every Reel posted on Facebook appears on the website automatically.

🚀 Option A: Built-in Website "Reels Manager" (/admin/reels)

Works just like the existing Gallery Manager (/admin/gallery) and Menu Manager (/admin/menus) — no GitHub login or HTML embed codes required.

1 Post the Reel on Facebook as Normal

Create and publish the Reel on The Ship Inn Porlock Weir Facebook Page from a phone or computer as usual.

2 Copy the Link from Facebook
  • On a Phone (Facebook App): Open the Reel → tap the Share arrow icon → tap Copy Link.
  • On a Computer: Open the Reel and copy the web address (or click Share → Copy Link).
3 Paste into the Website's Reels Manager

Open https://www.theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk/admin/reels (can be saved as an icon on the phone's home screen), paste the link into the box, optionally add a short caption, and tap "Publish Reel". It goes live within ~60 seconds.

4 Removing an Old Reel (Optional)

Scroll down to the Current Website Reels list on /admin/reels and tap 🗑️ Delete next to any Reel to remove it.

✨ Bonus: Pasting Reels Inside News & Events

With Option A, we can also make the News & Events editor automatically detect Facebook links. If the customer pastes a Facebook Reel link into any News or Event post in Outstatic CMS, it will automatically turn into an interactive video player inside that article.

🔄 Option B: Automatic Facebook Sync (3rd-Party Feed Widget)

Uses a social feed service (such as Curator.io, Behold, Elfsight, or SociableKIT) connected directly to The Ship Inn's Facebook Page.

1 One-Time Setup (~2 Minutes)

The customer clicks an invitation link you send them, clicks "Continue with Facebook", ticks The Ship Inn Porlock Weir page, and clicks Save.

2 Day-to-Day Usage (Zero Website Effort)

They never need to open the website to add Reels. Every time they post a Reel to The Ship Inn Facebook Page, it automatically appears on the website within 15–60 minutes.

3 Occasional Re-connection

If the customer changes their personal Facebook password, Facebook security disconnects third-party apps. They will receive an email link to click "Reconnect Facebook" (takes 30 seconds).

⚠️ 3 Facebook Rules the Customer Must Know (Applies to Both Options)
  1. Privacy Must Be "Public" (🌐): Reels posted to The Ship Inn Facebook Page are Public by default. Personal profile posts set to "Friends Only" cannot be shown on external websites.
  2. Commercial Chart Music May Be Muted Outside Facebook: Original audio (talking, live music in the bar, harbour sounds) plays with full sound on the website. Commercial pop songs added from Facebook's music sticker library may be muted by Facebook on external websites due to music licensing until the viewer clicks "Watch on Facebook".
  3. Allow Embedding Must Stay On: Enabled by default on all Facebook Business Pages.
