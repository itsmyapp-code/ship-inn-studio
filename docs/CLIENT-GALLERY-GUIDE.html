The Ship Inn Porlock Weir — Client Quick Guide

💡 Instant Live Sync Photos uploaded via the Gallery Manager tool are automatically converted into Outstatic CMS documents and committed directly to the website.

🚀 Method 1: Using the Gallery Manager (1-Minute Method)

1 Open the Gallery Manager

In your browser, visit: https://www.theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk/admin/gallery

2 Choose Upload Mode
  • Single Photo Upload: Add an individual photo with a custom title and caption.
  • Batch Upload: Upload 3–10 photos at once into a chosen category.
3 Select Category

Click the button that matches where the photo belongs (🍽️ Food & Drink, 🪵 Interior & Bar, 🏡 Exterior & Garden, 🛏️ Rooms & Cabins, 🌿 Surroundings, or Custom).

4 Choose Image & Enter Details

Click "Browse Image" to pick your photo (.webp, .jpg, .png, .avif). Check the live thumbnail preview and enter a short descriptive title.

5 Click "Upload & Publish Image"

Within 1 to 2 minutes, the live website will be updated with your new photo!

🔄 How Does This Connect to Outstatic CMS?

All photos uploaded via /admin/gallery are automatically added to your Outstatic Gallery collection:

  • Hide a Photo Temporarily: In Outstatic (/outstatic/gallery), change Status from Published to Draft and click Save.
  • Edit Captions or Titles: Click any photo in Outstatic to adjust text.
  • Delete a Photo: Use the 🗑️ Delete button in the Gallery Manager or in Outstatic.
