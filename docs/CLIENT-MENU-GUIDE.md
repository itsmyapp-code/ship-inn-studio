# The Ship Inn, Porlock Weir — Client Guide: How to Upload & Manage Menus

This guide explains how to upload, update, and manage your downloadable PDF menus on The Ship Inn website.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### ⚠️ File Format Requirement
> Downloadable menus **must be in `.pdf` format**.  
> If your menu is currently in Word (`.docx`), Apple Pages, or Canva, please export or save it as a PDF before uploading.

---

## 🚀 Method 1: Uploading a New Menu PDF (1-Minute Method)

Use the dedicated **Menu Manager** tool on your website:

1. **Open the Menu Uploader:**  
   In your browser, go to: `https://www.theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk/admin/menus`
2. **Select the Menu to Update:**  
   Click the button for the menu you are changing (**Breakfast Menu**, **Lunch Menu**, **Evening Menu**, **Sunday Lunch Menu**, or **Custom Menu**).
3. **Set the Season / Subtitle:**  
   Enter the season or serving times (e.g., `Autumn 2026` or `Served 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM`).
4. **Choose your PDF file:**  
   Click **"Browse PDF file"** and choose your `.pdf` file from your computer.
5. **Click "Upload & Publish Menu":**  
   Within **1 to 2 minutes**, the live website will be updated with your new downloadable PDF!

---

## 🔄 How Does This Connect to the Outstatic Menus Page?

The **Outstatic Menus** dashboard and the **Menu Uploader** work together automatically:

* **Automatic Synchronization:** Whenever you upload a PDF at `/admin/menus`, it automatically updates the corresponding document in your **Outstatic Menus** dashboard.
* **What you can do in Outstatic (`/outstatic/menus`):**
  * **Temporarily Hide a Menu:** Click on the menu and change **Status** from `Published` to `Draft`, then click **Save**. This hides it from the website immediately without deleting it.
  * **Change Display Order:** Adjust the *Display Order* number (e.g. `1` for Breakfast, `2` for Lunch, `3` for Evening, `4` for Sunday).
  * **Edit Descriptions & Notes:** Update internal notes or menu descriptions.

---
