Website Management Guide

The Ship Inn Porlock Weir • Admin Tools & Outstatic CMS

⚡ Fast Track: Which Tool to Use?

We have built dedicated 1-minute admin tools for the most frequent website tasks:

  • To Upload or Update PDF Menus: Use the Menu Manager (/admin/menus)
  • To Add or Remove Gallery Photos: Use the Gallery Manager (/admin/gallery)
  • To Edit Page Text, Hours, Contact Info, News, or Events: Use the Outstatic CMS (/outstatic)

1. Dedicated Fast Admin Tools (1-Minute Updates)

1.1 Menu Manager & PDF Uploader

Use this tool whenever you have a new season or updated menu PDF.

  1. Open your browser and visit: theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk/admin/menus
  2. Click the menu you want to update (Breakfast Menu, Lunch Menu, Evening Menu, Sunday Lunch Menu, or Custom Menu).
  3. Enter the Season / Subtitle (e.g., Autumn 2026 or Served 12:00 PM – 3:00 PM).
  4. Click "Browse PDF file" and pick your .pdf document.
  5. Click "Upload & Publish Menu". The website and Outstatic CMS will update automatically in 1–2 minutes!

1.2 Gallery Manager & Photo Uploader

Use this tool to add new photos to the website gallery or remove old ones without manual file coding.

  1. Open your browser and visit: theshipinnporlockweir.co.uk/admin/gallery
  2. Choose your mode:
    • Single Photo Upload: Add a photo with a title and optional caption.
    • Batch Upload: Drop 3–10 photos at once into a chosen category.
  3. Click your target category (🍽️ Food & Drink, 🪵 Interior & Bar, 🏡 Exterior & Garden, 🛏️ Rooms & Cabins, 🌿 Surroundings, or Custom).
  4. Click "Browse Image" to pick your photo (supports WebP, JPG, PNG, AVIF) and check the instant preview thumbnail.
  5. Enter a Photo Title and optional Caption.
  6. Click "Upload & Publish Image". The photo is published and synchronized with Outstatic immediately!

3. Understanding the Outstatic Dashboard

The left sidebar provides access to all content sections:

  • Pages — Controls text and banners on core website pages (Home, Food & Drink, Contact, Rooms, etc.).
  • Menus — Shows your uploaded menus (where you can toggle Draft/Published to temporarily hide a menu).
  • Gallery — Shows your gallery photo collection (where you can edit captions or toggle Draft/Published).
  • Events — Add upcoming live music, pub quizzes, and seasonal celebrations.
  • News — Post articles and announcements.
  • Media Library — Central repository for all images used on website pages.

4. How to Edit and Save Any Page

  1. In the left sidebar, click Pages, then click the page you want to edit.
  2. Ignore the large centre text editor for Pages (it is not used on the live layout).
  3. Scroll down the Right Sidebar to find all the customizable fields that control the website layout.
  4. If a field shows + Create, click it to open the input box, then type your updated text.
  5. Ensure Status is set to "Published".
  6. Click the black Save button (top-right). Changes go live within 1–2 minutes!

6. How to Add a New Event or News Article

  1. Click Events or News in the left sidebar of Outstatic.
  2. Click the black "New" button (top right).
  3. In the centre screen: Type your headline in the Title box, then write the story or event description below it.
  4. In the right sidebar: Pick the Date, add a Cover Image, and enter a short Description.
  5. Change Status to "Published" and click Save.
